About

My story

Hi, my name is Jesse Langley. Here is where I come from.

Born and raised

New Bern, North Carolina

Jesse Langley was born July 5th, 1987, in New Bern, North Carolina. Small-town America, where his late father served at Marine Corps Air Station Cherry Point until his retirement in 1998.

He had a good upbringing. Hard work, honesty, morals and values were all key ethics instilled in the family.

Country living

Rural life and summer jobs

The family lived rurally. Hunting and fishing became favorite pastimes, as well as tending to the livestock and hounds.

During the summers Jesse often mowed multiple lawns as a part-time job of his own, and he also worked on hurricane cleanup.

Language arts

Where the writing began

In high school Jesse was relatively successful in language arts, which is where he says his passion for writing began. He excelled at poetry.

His schooling still focused heavily on a military route. He attended and completed the four-year NJROTC program, and after his sophomore year he shipped out to Fort Benning, Georgia, for Army National Guard Basic Training. Read more on the Journey page.

Music interest and development

The sound of the 90s and early 2000s

Growing up in the 90s and the early 2000s was instrumental in Jesse's development, as he was exposed to so many genres, artists and bands. Artists like Eminem, and bands like Linkin Park, P.O.D, Three Days Grace and Shinedown, heavily impacted his aspirations as a lyricist and songwriter.

His first debut song, I Wish I, was fully written in 2002 after a high school breakup. He used to carry around a red three-ring binder full of the songs he wrote in high school.
