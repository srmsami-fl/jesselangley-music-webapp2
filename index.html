My story

Hi, my name is Jesse Langley

I was born on July 5th, 1987, in New Bern, North Carolina. Small-town America, where my late father served at Marine Corps Air Station Cherry Point until his retirement in 1998.

Hard work, honesty, morals and values were the key ethics instilled in our upbringing. In high school I found my passion for writing, excelling at poetry, and my first song was fully written in 2002 after a high school breakup.

The first song

"I Wish I"

Jesse's debut song, I Wish I, was fully written in 2002 after a high school breakup.

He used to carry around a red three-ring binder full of the songs he wrote in high school. It was the start of a lifelong habit of turning real life into lyrics.
