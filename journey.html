The journey

School, service, work

The jobs and the discipline behind the songs.

Service

NJROTC and the Army National Guard

Jesse's schooling focused heavily on a military route. He attended and completed the four-year NJROTC program.

After his sophomore year he shipped out to Fort Benning, Georgia, for Basic Training as part of his Army National Guard contract, as an E-3 (Private First Class) with 92 Yankee, Supply Specialist, as his classification.

After graduating from Basic Training he returned to graduate high school, and he went on to complete a four-year contract with the Army.

NJROTC, 4 yearsE-3 Private First Class92 Yankee

Career, part one

The fast food years

Throughout his late teens to mid 20s, Jesse specialized in the fast food industry. He earned ServSafe certifications for multiple well-known companies, and held a couple of assistant manager positions in the latter years.

At 29 he transitioned out of the industry to focus on a career development pathway.

Career, part two

Finding HVAC

That is when Jesse found, and fell in love with, HVAC. He started at bare minimum entry-level pay and worked his way diligently through the ranks, becoming a lead installer within two years.

He loved everyone there, from the managers to his co-workers. It genuinely felt like a big family to him.

Jesse worked in HVAC for nine years, until a career-ending stroke.
